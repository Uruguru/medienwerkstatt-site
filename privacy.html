Privacy Policy

This policy explains the data handled by the Medienwerkstatt website and by authorised YouTube and TikTok connections in the local application.

1. Controller and contact

The operator identified on the contact and provider information page is responsible for processing. Privacy questions can be sent to hmnprjct1@gmail.com.

2. This website

This is a static information website hosted with GitHub Pages. It has no user accounts, forms, analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. When a page is requested, GitHub may process technical connection data such as the IP address, request time, requested file, browser information and referring page to deliver and protect the service. GitHub's own privacy documentation applies to that hosting activity.

3. TikTok connection

When an authorised operator connects a TikTok account, Medienwerkstatt requests only the permissions required for the selected publishing mode:

  • user.info.basic to identify the connected TikTok account;
  • video.upload to transfer an approved video to the TikTok inbox for completion by the account holder;
  • video.publish only when Direct Post has been separately configured and authorised.

The application processes the connected account identifier and display name, OAuth tokens, the approved video file, its caption and the status or identifier returned by TikTok. It does not request TikTok passwords, viewer identities, private messages or contact lists.

4. YouTube connection

For authorised YouTube channels, Medienwerkstatt can use youtube.upload to upload approved videos and youtube.readonly to identify the connected channel and read public performance figures for videos managed by the workflow. It does not read message content, viewer identities or subscriber lists.

5. Purpose and storage

Account information is used to display and verify the selected destination. Video files and metadata are processed to perform the publishing action chosen by the operator. Status information prevents duplicate transfers and records whether a platform accepted the request.

OAuth credentials and operational records are stored locally in a protected location outside the source-code repository. Tokens are retained while the connection is active and deleted locally when the account is disconnected. Production and status records are kept only as long as needed to operate and document the related media workflow.

6. Recipients and platform terms

Data is sent to TikTok or YouTube only when the corresponding connection or publishing action is used. Their services process data under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

No connected-account data is sold. YouTube or TikTok account information is not sent to OpenAI.

7. Revocation and deletion

Platform access can be revoked in the connected platform's account settings. The operator can also disconnect the account in Medienwerkstatt, which removes the locally stored token. Requests concerning locally held personal data can be sent to the contact address above.

8. Updates

This policy will be updated when the website, requested platform permissions or data handling changes.
