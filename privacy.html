Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Summary

Medienwerkstatt is a private tool used only by its operator to publish videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else.

2. YouTube API Services

Medienwerkstatt uses YouTube API Services. Google's privacy policy applies to data processed by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Use of the tool is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

3. What data is accessed

With the operator's permission (Google OAuth), Medienwerkstatt accesses only the operator's own YouTube channels:

  • Upload (youtube.upload): to upload videos with title, description and tags.
  • Read (youtube.readonly): channel name and ID, and public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) of videos uploaded by the tool.

It does not read comments' content, viewers' identities, subscriber lists, messages, or any data of other channels or users.

4. How the data is used

Channel name and ID are used to show which channel is connected. Video statistics are used to decide which kinds of stories to produce more often. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling of people.

5. Storage

All data, including OAuth access and refresh tokens, is stored only locally on the operator's computer in a protected folder outside of any code repository. No data is stored on external servers by Medienwerkstatt. Video statistics are kept as long as the corresponding video exists and are deleted together with the local project data. Tokens are deleted when a channel is disconnected.

6. Sharing

Data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party. Videos and their metadata are, by design, sent to YouTube for publishing. Story texts are generated with the OpenAI API; no YouTube user data is sent to OpenAI.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Medienwerkstatt itself does not place cookies on any viewer's device.

8. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation the stored tokens no longer work and are deleted locally.
