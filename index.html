Local production · deliberate publishing

From source material to a reviewed short-form video.

Medienwerkstatt is a desktop workflow for creating, checking and publishing vertical videos to authorised media channels. Production files and account credentials remain under the operator's control.

How it works Read the Privacy Policy

What it does

One controlled media workflow

01

Produce

Prepare short-form videos from authorised media, narration, subtitles and locally managed assets.

02

Review

Check the final video, destination account, caption, disclosures and publishing settings before release.

03

Publish

Transfer approved videos through official platform interfaces or hand them to the platform inbox for completion.

Platform connections

Only the permissions needed for the selected action

Medienwerkstatt can connect authorised YouTube and TikTok accounts. TikTok connections use basic account information and, when enabled, permission to upload a video to the user's TikTok inbox. Direct publishing is used only when it has been separately configured and approved.

The website itself has no login, does not accept visitor uploads and does not use analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies.

Operator control

Nothing is published merely by visiting this website.

Publishing actions happen inside the local Medienwerkstatt application after account authorisation and an explicit release decision.
